About This Project

Student Productivity Hub is a simple and powerful tool designed to help students stay organized, focused, and productive in their daily studies.

Features

Notes

Write and save your important ideas and study notes easily.

To-Do List

Manage your daily tasks and track your progress efficiently.

Focus Mode

Stay distraction-free with a built-in focus timer.

Why I Built This

Many students struggle with managing time and staying consistent. This project was built to create a simple, distraction-free system that helps students focus on what truly matters.

Future Improvements

  • AI-powered note summarization
  • Smart task suggestions
  • Cloud sync across devices
